The loop

  1. Tap the base to launch the glass ball. It shatters and the ants spill out.
  2. Any swarm can eat anything; more ants eat it faster. Swarms that meet merge.
  3. Every object is worth some eggs, laid in batches at checkpoints as it is eaten. Eggs fly to the colony and hatch into the next ball.
  4. Ants never die. A swarm with nothing nearby slowly looks further out until it finds food.
  5. Eat the three objectives in red rings before the clock runs out.
