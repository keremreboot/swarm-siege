The loop

  1. Tap the island to launch the glass ball. It shatters and the ants spill out.
  2. Every piece of food takes a number of ants to lift; food that takes three or more shows the number over it, green when the next ball has enough.
  3. A swarm only goes for food it has the ants for, and sends exactly that many; the rest look for more. Swarms with nothing to do merge when they meet.
  4. Carriers haul their food to the nearest ant hole. Down it goes: the carriers come back into the cannon, with new ants hatched from what they brought.
  5. Ants never die. A swarm with nothing it can lift nearby waits a moment, then walks to a hole and rejoins the ball.
  6. With the free ant on, the colony also hatches one ant every 15 seconds; its rim fills toward the next one.
  7. Carry off the objectives in red rings before the clock runs out. Each chip shows how many ants it takes, and turns green when the next ball has them.
  8. In Sweet stacks every piece is a real object, carried off from the top down. A ball landing close shoves the stacks, and can topple one.
