The loop

  1. Tap the base to launch the glass ball. It shatters and the ants spill out.
  2. A swarm eats anything whose number is at or below its size. Swarms that meet merge.
  3. Every bite flies to the colony, which hatches new ants into the next ball.
  4. Ants only live a short while once they land. The bar under a swarm's count shows how long it has left. Ants waiting in the ball don't age.
  5. Eat the three red objectives before the clock runs out.
